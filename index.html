Our Story

The Ochola family has positively impacted many lives throughout its dynasty — from education support to medical care assistance and financial support. Across generations, the family has held to one compassionate approach: showing up for the community and outreaching in society.

As heirs to this work, the Ochola grandchildren carry the legacy forward through our own generation, creating more avenues to support families and communities in need. It is the same spirit our loving grandmother instilled in all of us, kept alive in our hands.

Where We Show Up

Education

Supporting school fees, supplies, and opportunities for young people to learn.

Medical Care

Helping families reach and afford the care they need, when they need it.

Financial Support

Standing behind families and community members through direct assistance.
